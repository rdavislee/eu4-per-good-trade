## Notes for whoever fixes these

- **Four of the five refutations and eight of the partials are one defect**: a figure measured
  before §2.3's tie-break and pinned tolerance went in, and not re-measured after. `Y538` (cloves
  to Deccan), `Y152` (46/16), `Y154` (30 flips), and the counts in `Y143`, `Y1032` and `Y1055` are
  all of this kind. `final.py` — the harness that produces the §3.13 calibration figures §2.8
  quotes — still calls `mincost_flow(b, zeros)` with **no cost argument**, i.e. unit arc costs
  (`final.py` line 240). Re-pointing it at `TIE_COST` would be the single highest-value repair, and
  it would also make `Y1034`'s "no figure moved" true of the calibration rows and not just the
  baseline.
- **`Y1038` is a live risk, not a bookkeeping error.** The document tells an implementer that
  changing the normalisation moves "a handful of per-good graphs" and nothing aggregate. Under the
  shipped solver it moves **13** per-good graphs *and* **7 of 159** aggregate edges. That is advice
  pointing the wrong way, and the reason it went wrong is instructive: the normalisation set that
  produced the published figure contains `/total*N`, which is `/mean` written differently, so the
  world-total case — the only one of the three that actually moves the aggregate — was never in the
  experiment. Any re-measurement should assert the normalisations are pairwise distinct as vectors
  before trusting the diff, and should run at `LP_OPTS`.

- **The three counts that are sample statistics** (`Y1032`'s 12-and-8, `Y1055`'s 8, `Y143`'s 84 and
  13) cannot be made reproducible by re-measuring; they need either a stated seed or replacement by
  the invariant they are evidence for ("only `copper` and `paper` move at the default tolerance;
  none moves at 1e-10").
- **`Y539` and `Y538` are one mistake with two heads: reading `spice` as `spices`.** v2 wrote about
  the spice *class* and named the member in a parenthetical; v6.1 read the parenthetical as a
  contradiction, retracted a true sentence, and then asserted the calibration puts cloves in Deccan
  when v2 had it right at Beijing all along. Fixing `Y538` to `beijing` and dropping the `Y539`
  retraction are the same repair, and the safest wording distinguishes the good from the class
  explicitly.
- **Two internal contradictions** worth closing: §2.4 says the cycle crash was "reproduced on three
  launches" (right — three dumps) while §2.7 says "twice" (`Y518`); and §2.8 says the calibration
  moves cloves to Deccan while §3.13 says the calibrated cloves demand order is
  `hangzhou, beijing, doab` (`Y538`).
- **Two claims are stronger than the document says.** `Y1021`'s quantisation is 3,354 of 3,354, not
  495 of 495 — but it is also a property of the save writer's fixed 3-decimal format rather than of
  the trade code, which `Y1023` already concedes. And `Y479`'s withdrawal of the widest-band
  argument is *more* creditable than it looks: the α = 2 band **is** still the widest on [1, 8]
  (1.63-3.28, width 1.65), so the argument was withdrawn on principle rather than because it had
  stopped working.
