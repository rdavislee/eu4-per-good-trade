## What to produce

A complete census of the document's claims, as a markdown file at
`../claims-v6.md`.

One row per claim. A claim is any statement the document makes that could be true or false: a
numeric figure, a mechanism assertion, a statement about what the game engine does, a statement about
what the model does, a citation of a file or a script.

For each claim record:

- **ID** — the ID prefix for this version is `Y`. Rounds 10 and 11 issued up to `Y1050`, so the
  three-digit range is full: **use four digits from `Y1000` onward** for new claims. `Y001`-`Y999`
  keep the meanings they already have. This is the twelfth inventory of this version's document. The
  previous one is `scripts/claims-v6-round11.md`, a full census of 1,042 claims. **Keep an ID attached to the same claim across
  inventories**: if a claim was `Y083` last time and is still in the document, it is `Y083` now, even
  if its wording changed. Give new claims unused numbers. Do not renumber to close gaps.
- **Section** — the `§` number it appears under.
- **Claim** — the statement, in your words, one sentence.
- **Status** — `UNCHANGED` (present in v5 with the same content), `CHANGED` (present in v5, content
  differs), `NEW` (not in v5), `REMOVED` (in v5, not here — list these too).
- **Type** — `ENGINE` (asserts something about how EU4 behaves), `MODEL` (asserts something about
  what this mod's design or its scripts do), `INSTALL` (asserts the content of a file in the game
  install), `MATH` (an algebraic or numerical identity), `DESIGN` (a stated intent or preference).
- **Provenance** — how the document says it knows. `read from a file` / `computed by a named script`
  / `measured in-game` / `numerical test` / `engine test` / `algebraic derivation` / `stipulated`
  (asserted as a design choice, no evidence claimed) / `unsourced` (asserts a fact and cites nothing).
  A **numerical test** is a computation over the model's own data; an **engine test** is an
  observation of the running game. They are different provenances and must not be merged.
- **Locator** — the line number or a short quoted fragment, enough to find it again.

Then a short summary: counts by Status, by Type, by Provenance; how many IDs carried over; which v5
claims are gone.

## What this task is and is not

This is enumeration, not evaluation. Record what the document claims and where it says the claim
comes from. Do not assess whether a claim is true, whether the document is consistent with itself,
whether a figure looks stale, or whether the writing is good. Another agent does that separately, and
if you pre-judge here it will inherit your guesses instead of testing the document.

If a claim's provenance is genuinely ambiguous — the document states a figure and names a script, but
the script's name does not appear near the figure, say — record the ambiguity in the Provenance cell
as a fact about the document ("figure stated at line 412; nearest script citation is six lines
earlier and covers a different quantity"). That is still enumeration: you are describing the page.
