# Addendum — solver-tolerance exposure in §3.x (run after the report, at the coordinator's request)

Part 3 found that an unpinned LP tolerance **undercounts** configuration differences rather than
inflating them, because two genuinely different optima can return the same suboptimal vertex. The
question was whether any §3.x figure is exposed the same way. **Two figures in my slice are
difference counts off an LP, and both were re-measured at each tolerance. Neither is exposed.**

| figure (§3.6, Y656) | pinned 1e-10 | HiGHS default 1e-7 |
|---|---|---|
| support-membership changes, 29 goods x 6 nudges of 1e-9 | 6 changes, max flow **1.16e-9**, 1 good | 6 changes, max flow **1.16e-9**, 1 good |
| edges moved under +/-1% wealth noise, 6 seeds | **[0, 0, 0, 0, 0, 0]**, 0 sink-set changes | **[0, 0, 0, 0, 0, 0]**, 0 sink-set changes |

**Why they are safe, and where the exposure actually lives.** On the shipped field the aggregate
solve's uniqueness margin is **7.527e-06**, about 75x HiGHS's 1e-7 default, so the default cannot
land on a wrong vertex on `b_w`, and every aggregate difference count (the wealth-noise zero, the
relabelling result) is out of reach of the effect.

**Correction to the paragraph above — that 75x is an accident of `alpha_Phi = 2.0`, not a property
of the operator.** Measured across the exponent:

| `alpha_Phi` | 1.0 | 1.5 | 2.0 | 2.5 | 3.0 | 4.0 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| aggregate margin | 4.937e-07 | **1.267e-07** | 7.527e-06 | **1.267e-07** | **1.267e-07** | 4.127e-07 |
| headroom over the 1e-7 default | 5x | **1.3x** | 75x | **1.3x** | **1.3x** | 4x |

At **`alpha_Phi = 1.5`, the value v6.0 shipped one version ago, the margin is 1.267e-07 — 1.3x the
default, effectively sitting on the tolerance.** The 59-fold jump came from a hyperparameter the
document itself moved this version (§0: `alpha_Phi` 1.5 -> 2.0). So the headroom is not banked, and
the trigger for re-measuring is **not only a solver change**: any move in `alpha_Phi`, in the wealth
model, or in the field can put the aggregate margin back inside the default. (Under +/-1% wealth
noise at the shipped exponent it is stable at 7.527e-06 on all six seeds, so it is the exponent that
moves it, not small perturbations.)

**How much headroom the *pinned* tolerance has, as opposed to the default.** Over 124 solves — 4
per-good exponents x 29 goods, 7 `alpha_Phi` values, and 8 seeds of +/-5% wealth noise — the
smallest minimum-positive off-support reduced cost seen anywhere is **2.498e-08** (`copper` at
k = 1.5), which is **250x** the pinned 1e-10. So the exposure is entirely to HiGHS's 1e-7 *default*,
not to the pinned setting: nothing on any field I could reach comes near 1e-10. This also explains retroactively why pinning the tolerance was a
correctness requirement rather than a performance knob: at v6.0's exponent the aggregate was already
on the line, not 75x clear of it. **Two** per-good margins fall inside the default — `copper`
**3.765e-08** and `paper` **8.915e-08**, and only those two; the other **27 of 29** sit outside it
(next is `cotton` at 1.177e-07). So the exposed set is those two solves, not the per-good class —
but §3.6's per-good test perturbs demand by 1e-9, three orders below the margin, far too small to
move a vertex either way. That is why the counts coincide rather than the tolerance not mattering.

**The one place the exposure would have bitten is being deleted anyway.** §3.13's calibration
figures are per-good difference counts measured at **unit cost** and inherited from v5.0 —
`drain-orientation.md` §6's "5 of 29 sink sets shifted" and §5's "5.8 of 79 flow arcs per good".
Those are exactly the shape at risk, and dropping them (agreed repair for Y739/Y741) closes the
exposure rather than merely tidying the prose.

**A checkable prediction for `final.py`'s re-point.** `final.py` inherits the pinned `LP_OPTS` but
calls `mincost_flow(b, zeros)` with `cost=None`, i.e. **unit costs**. Its `V035` — "orientation
flips under 2 index permutations x 29 goods" — is therefore a per-good flip count under the
degenerate objective. Re-pointing it at `TIE_COST` should take `V035` to **0**: `relabel6.py`
reports 40 of 40 permutations returning a different support at unit cost against 0 of 40 with the
tie-break, and my per-good runs show the same collapse (15/15 -> 0/15). If `V035` does not go to
zero after the re-point, the re-point did not land.
