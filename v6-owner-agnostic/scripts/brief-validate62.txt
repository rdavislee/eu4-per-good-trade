## Method — this defines the verdicts

- Re-derive; never inherit. A claim graded in any earlier round is graded again from sources.
- Read the file, not the document's quotation of it. Where a claim cites a game file, open the file.
- Re-run measurements. Where a claim states a measured figure, produce the figure. Where a
  claim's instrument exists, run it; where the instrument disagrees with the document, the
  instrument's output is the evidence and the disagreement is the finding.
- Attack derivations as arguments. A derivation is graded by checking each step, not by
  re-measuring its conclusion. Keep measurement-verdicts and derivation-verdicts distinct in
  your Method column.
- A universal is graded as a universal. A claim proved or asserted for any input, checked only
  on the 1444 start, is at most PARTIAL; a proof that holds on 1444 but fails on a constructible
  input is REFUTED.
- No confirmation on plausibility, consensus, or community documentation. A wiki is not a
  source. A claim that cannot be settled with the materials above is UNTESTABLE, and its row
  states exactly what is missing.
- Scope is yours. Grade what the sentence asserts, including its qualifiers and its stated
  scope — not what a friendlier reading of it would assert.

Type is a hint, not a rule: ENGINE claims live in game files/saves; MEASURED claims carry a
figure to reproduce; MODEL claims describe the design's algorithm/mathematics — grade them
against the reference implementation and by checking the mathematics; DESIGN claims are
stipulations or intent — grade the checkable content (that the implementation and the rest of
the document actually conform to the stipulation); where a sentence asserts pure intent with
nothing checkable in any source, it is UNTESTABLE and the row says that is the gap. PROCESS
claims are about the project's own files and history — open the named files, diff the versions.

## Verdicts

- CONFIRMED  — the sources establish the claim as stated.
- PARTIAL    — part holds, part does not, or the claim outruns its evidence in scope or
               precision. State which part fails.
- REFUTED    — the sources contradict the claim. For a figure, report the value the sources give.
- UNTESTABLE — cannot be settled with the materials above. State what would settle it.
