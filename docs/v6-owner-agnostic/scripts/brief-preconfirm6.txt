## What to produce

`C:\Users\rdavi\OneDrive\Documents\Paradox Interactive\Europa Universalis IV\mod\per-good-trade\v6-owner-agnostic\preconfirm-round6.md`, one block per row ID (A1-A5, B1-B19, C1-C13, D1-D10):

- **ID** and **verdict**: `CONFIRMED`, `WRONG`, or `UNMEASURABLE`
- **Method** — the command you ran or the computation you performed
- **Value** — what you got, against what the row claims

Then a summary: counts by verdict, and the list of WRONG rows with the correct value for each.

## How to measure

Measure independently. Where the list names a figure, compute it yourself rather than reading it out
of a script's cached output or from the validation reports that produced it. Where a row proposes a
change to a script, check that the change does what the row says and that nothing else in the tree
depends on the current behaviour.

Several rows record figures that a previous measurement got wrong, and say so. Treat those as
unverified claims like any other — the correction may itself be wrong.

Three rows carry a specific warning about how to measure them, in the file's closing section. Those
warnings are there because a previous attempt failed in exactly the way described; read them before
measuring those rows, and check that the failure mode described is real rather than taking it on
faith.

Where a figure is a sample over random draws, say so and give the spread rather than a point value.
Where a figure cannot be measured from any source available to you, say `UNMEASURABLE` and name the
source that would settle it.

Report what you find, including where the list is right.
